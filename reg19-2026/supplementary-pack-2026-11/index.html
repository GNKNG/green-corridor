Swindon Urban Green Network - Supplementary Evidence Pack (23 Nov 2026)

Public supplementary evidence supporting the Whole-Area Green Infrastructure Network representation (Swindon Local Plan 2043 Regulation 19, filed 12 November 2026). Prepared for Council officer review and the examination stage, alongside updated interactive viewer layers (23 Nov 2026 baseline).

Open updated interactive draft map All project links Original filing (12 Nov 2026 PDF)

Headline summary (Swindon five urban review zones, 6,462.8 ha)

  • Whole combined network: 1,602.3 ha (24.8% of the urban area) across three clearly separated tiers: 785.9 ha (12.2%) adopted EN1 corridors, 81.4 ha (1.3%) other existing green space outside EN1, and 735.0 ha (11.4%) net proposed parcels completing the network on the ground.
  • Nine parcel types (1 to 6b): classifies existing vegetated links (wooded belts, mixed/linear strips, amenity grass, hedgerows, wetlands, institutional/campus grounds, water margins) and their realistic management needs.
  • Part 2 parcel schedule: 1,165 whole proposed parcels (PR-0001 to PR-1208, 43 historic IDs retired) across 1,474 typed segments Swindon-wide (763.0 ha gross Swindon-wide; 1,465 unique segments / 735.9 ha gross inside the five urban zones).
