# Regulation 19 representations — filed 12 November 2026

The representation below was submitted to Swindon Borough Council by email on 12 November 2026, within the Regulation 19 publication period for the Swindon Local Plan 2043. It is published here so that councillors, officers and residents can read it without going through the consultation portal.

## `whole-area-green-network-2026-11-12.pdf` — 25 pages

Policy CSE7 protects the Green Infrastructure Network *"as defined on the Policies Map"*, so the map decides where the policy applies. The adopted EN1 layer records 40 corridors covering about 900 hectares — parks, woodland and the larger open spaces — but not the smaller green ground connecting them: verges, hedgerow runs, wooded strips, railway margins, the green between streets.

The representation supplies that ground as a mapped layer. Within the Council's own urban envelope it takes the recorded network from **908 hectares to 1,648**, and from 14.0 per cent of the built-up area to 25.4. One amendment, applied once, rather than several hundred separate parcel requests.

It also asks that the layer and its data note be published with the plan, and that the Council state what its records currently show and the reasoning applied where an area has been excluded.

## What is not claimed

- No new designation or consent regime; the request uses the policy the plan already carries.
- No blanket prohibition on development; mapping does not replace site-by-site assessment.
- No claim that the map is wrong about what it shows — the claim is that its coverage is incomplete.
- No claim about ownership or habitat quality.
