Help & Surveyor Guide

📱 Install as a Phone App (PWA)

You can install this map on your smartphone as a standalone app without going to an app store:

  • Android (Chrome / Firefox): Tap the ⋮ menu button → tap "Install app" or "Add to Home screen".
  • iPhone / iPad (Safari): Tap the Share button (box with up arrow) → scroll down → tap "Add to Home Screen".

Once installed, it opens full screen with its own green corridor icon and works completely offline.

📡 Real-Time GPS Tracking

When walking in the field, tap the GPS (◎) button on the left map toolbar. A pulsing blue dot with an accuracy ring will show your live position. Tap again to turn off and save battery.

📦 Offline Surveying & Map Packs

If you are walking in woodland or rural corridors with no phone signal:

  • Save View Offline: Before setting out, open Layers → Cache View to store map tiles for your route.
  • Offline Submissions: Any areas or pins you draw while offline will save safely to your phone storage. As soon as you reconnect to mobile data or Wi-Fi, they upload automatically.
  • Import Custom Packs: Tap Import Pack in the Layers menu to load local community .geojson or .json map files.

✎ How to Annotate & Submit

  • Area (▭): Tap the map to outline a green corridor or habitat area. Double-tap or click Finish to complete.
  • Marker (⚑): Tap the map to flag a specific habitat feature, veteran tree, pond, or verge.
  • Measure / Calc Area: Check boundary lengths and estimated area in hectares.
  • Photos: You can attach a photo of a habitat, veteran tree, or green space. All camera EXIF GPS location tags are automatically stripped on your phone before upload to protect wildlife and privacy.
  • Share a Space: Tap any green space or right of way on the map and click Share this space to copy a direct link or share straight to WhatsApp or email.

⚠ Protected Species & Badger Law

Under the Protection of Badgers Act 1992, it is a criminal offence to publish the precise location of a badger sett.

Any badger record submitted here is automatically snapped to Swindon Borough Council Civic Offices (Euclid Street) across the entire borough before leaving your phone. Precise coordinates are never stored or published.

Detailed confidential records of protected species should be submitted directly to Swindon Borough Council ecology officers.
